# Disciple.Tools | Deleting a Record

Deleting a record permanently removes it from your Disciple.Tools system. This action cannot be undone, so use it carefully. You might consider alternatives like archiving a contact or updating its status before deleting.

## Who Can Delete Records

Only users with the appropriate permissions can delete records. Typically, this includes administrators and users with specific roles that have been granted delete permissions for the record type.

## How to Delete a Record

To permanently delete a record:

1. Navigate to the record you want to delete.
2. In the **Admin Actions** dropdown at the top of the page, click to open the menu.
3. Select **Delete Record** from the list of options. This will open a confirmation dialog.
4. The dialog will display the record's name and ask "Are you sure you want to delete [record name]?"
5. Review the confirmation message carefully. Remember, deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.
6. If you are certain you want to delete the record, click the **Delete** button.
7. If you change your mind, click the **Cancel** button or close the dialog to exit without deleting.

Once confirmed, the record will be immediately and permanently deleted from the system. You will be redirected away from the deleted record.

## Important Considerations

- **Deletion is permanent**: Once a record is deleted, it cannot be recovered. All information, connections, and history associated with the record will be lost.
- **Consider alternatives**: Before deleting, consider whether archiving the record or changing its status might be more appropriate. These options preserve the data while removing it from active use.
- **Connected records**: Deleting a record will remove its connections to other records, but it will not delete those connected records themselves.
- **Permissions required**: If you do not see the Delete Record option in the Admin Actions menu, you do not have permission to delete records. Contact your system administrator if you believe you need this permission.

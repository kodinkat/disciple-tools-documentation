# Disciple.Tools | Archiving a Record

Archiving a contact removes it from active lists and workflows while preserving all its data. This is useful for contacts that are no longer active but whose information you want to keep for reference. Unlike deleting, archiving is reversible—you can restore an archived contact to active status at any time.

## When to Use Archive

Archiving is a natural part of the contact lifecycle and is helpful for any contact type. You should consider archiving a contact when:

- There is no action needed on your part for this contact
- The contact has gone through initial activity but is no longer requiring active engagement
- The relationship or outreach has concluded, but you want to preserve the contact's information and history
- The contact is inactive, but you may need to reference them in the future

Archiving is not limited to specific contact types—it is a standard workflow for managing all contacts in Disciple.Tools. Archiving removes the contact from your active work lists but keeps all the contact's history, connections, and information intact.

## How to Archive a Record

There are two ways to archive a contact in Disciple.Tools:

### Method 1: Using the Status Field (Main Method)

This is the primary way to archive most contacts:

1. Navigate to the contact record you want to archive.
2. Locate the **Status** field on the record (typically in the Details or Status tile).
3. Click on the current status to open the dropdown menu.
4. Select **Archived** from the list of status options.
5. The contact will be immediately archived and removed from active lists.

This method works for all contact types and is the standard way to archive contacts.

### Method 2: Using the Admin Actions Menu (For Personal and Placeholder Contacts)

This secondary method is only available for Personal and Placeholder contact types:

1. Navigate to the contact record you want to archive.
2. In the **Admin Actions** dropdown at the top of the page, click to open the menu.
3. Select **Archive Record** from the list of options. This will open a confirmation dialog.
4. The dialog will display the contact's name and ask "Are you sure you want to archive [contact name]?"
5. If you are certain you want to archive the contact, click the **Archive** button.
6. If you change your mind, click the **Cancel** button or close the dialog to exit without archiving.

Once confirmed, the contact will be moved to archived status.

**Note**: If you do not see the Archive Record option in the Admin Actions menu, the contact is likely a different type. You can still archive it using Method 1 (the Status field).

## How to Unarchive a Record

If you need to restore an archived contact to active status, you can use either method:

### Using the Status Field

1. Navigate to the archived contact record.
2. Locate the **Status** field on the record.
3. Click on **Archived** to open the dropdown menu.
4. Select a new active status (such as **Active** or any other appropriate status for your workflow).
5. The contact will be restored to active status and will reappear in your active work lists.

### Using the Unarchive Button

For Personal and Placeholder contacts:

1. Navigate to the archived contact record.
2. At the top of the page, you will see an archived notification banner.
3. Click the **Unarchive** button in the notification banner.
4. The contact will be restored to active status and will reappear in your active work lists.

## What Happens When You Archive

When a contact is archived:

- It is removed from active contact lists and filtered views
- All data, history, and connections are preserved
- The contact can still be accessed by searching for it or viewing archived contacts
- You can view and edit the archived contact's information
- The contact can be unarchived at any time to restore it to active status

## Archive vs. Delete

It is important to understand the difference:

- **Archive**: Removes the contact from active lists but preserves all data. The action is reversible—you can unarchive the contact later.
- **Delete**: Permanently removes the contact and all its data from the system. This action cannot be undone.

In most cases, archiving is the safer option when you are uncertain whether you will need the contact information in the future.

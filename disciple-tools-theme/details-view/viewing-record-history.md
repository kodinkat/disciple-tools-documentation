# Disciple.Tools | Viewing Record History

The Record History feature provides a complete, chronological log of every change made to a record since its creation. This includes field updates, status changes, connections added or removed, and other modifications. Record history is useful for tracking progress, understanding how a record has evolved, and auditing changes.

## How to View Record History

To access the complete history of a record:

1. Navigate to the record whose history you want to view.
2. In the **Admin Actions** dropdown at the top of the page, click to open the menu.
3. Select **View Record History** from the list of options. This will open the Record History modal.
4. The modal will display a chronological timeline of all changes made to the record, with the most recent changes typically shown first.

## Understanding the Record History Display

The Record History modal shows detailed information about each change:

- **What changed**: The field or property that was modified
- **Old and new values**: The previous value and the new value after the change
- **Who made the change**: The user who performed the action
- **When it changed**: The date and time stamp of the modification

## Filtering the History

The Record History modal includes filtering options to help you find specific changes:

### Filter by Date

On the left side of the modal, you will find a **Filter To Date** dropdown menu:

1. Click the dropdown to see a list of dates when changes were made to the record.
2. Select a specific date to view only the changes made on that day.
3. This helps you narrow down the history when looking for changes made during a particular time period.

### Show All Activities Toggle

Below the date filter, there is a **show all** toggle switch:

- When the switch is **on** (default): All types of changes are displayed in the history.
- When the switch is **off**: The history may be filtered to show only certain types of activities, depending on your system configuration.

## Closing the Record History

When you are finished reviewing the history, click the **X** button in the top right corner of the modal to close it and return to the record's details page.

## Who Can View Record History

Users with update permissions for a record can typically view its history. If you do not see the View Record History option in the Admin Actions menu, you may not have the necessary permissions for that record.
